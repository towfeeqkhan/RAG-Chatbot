# Voltway Refund and Return Policy

This policy covers hardware returns, charging session refunds, installation fees, and subscriptions.

## Hardware returns

- **Return window:** 30 days from delivery.
- **Refund amount:** Full refund, with no restocking fee, even if the product has been used.
- **Return label:** A prepaid return label is provided.
- **Refund timing:** Refunds go to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days of Voltway receiving the item.
- **Return address:** Voltway Returns Center, 4500 Industrial Blvd, Dallas, Texas 75247.

## Installation fee

The professional installation fee is refunded only if the installation was not yet performed.

## Charging session refunds

If a session fails or the charger delivers less than expected, request a refund in the Voltway app within 7 days.

- Refunds are issued as account credit.
- You can ask for the refund to go to your card instead.
- If a session charged you but delivered no energy, request a refund in the app within 7 days.

## Subscription refunds

- You can cancel anytime in the app.
- Monthly plans end at the end of the billing period.
- Annual plans are refunded pro rata within the first 30 days only.

See [subscription plans](../pricing/subscription-plans.md) for plan details.

## Quick reference

| Situation | Window | Refund |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hardware return | 30 days from delivery | Full refund, no restocking fee, to original payment method |
| Installation fee | Before installation | Refunded only if installation not yet performed |
| Failed charging session | 7 days | Account credit, or to card on request |
| Annual subscription | First 30 days | Pro rata |
| Monthly subscription | n/a | Ends at end of billing period |

# Voltway Shipping Policy

This policy covers shipping for Voltway hardware orders.

## Shipping costs

- **Free shipping** on hardware orders over $300. Voltway Home Pro 2 ($549), Voltway Home Lite ($349), and Voltway Go ($399) all qualify.
- **Orders under $300** pay $15.00 flat shipping.

## Delivery times

Delivery takes 3 to 7 business days within the contiguous United States.

## Where we ship

| Destination | Shipping available? |
| --- | --- |
| Contiguous United States | Yes |
| Alaska | No |
| Hawaii | No |
| Outside the US | No, at this time |

## Where hardware ships from

Hardware ships from the Dallas warehouse.

## Returns

Returned items go to the Voltway Returns Center, 4500 Industrial Blvd, Dallas, Texas 75247. See the [refund policy](refund-policy.md). You have 30 days from delivery, and a prepaid return label is provided.

## After delivery

If you plan to use a professional installer, scheduling typically takes 5 to 10 business days after ordering. See the [installation guide](../support/installation-guide.pdf).

Questions about an order? Contact support@voltway.example.

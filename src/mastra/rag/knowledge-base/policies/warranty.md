# Voltway Hardware Warranty

All Voltway home charging hardware is covered by a warranty against manufacturing defects. This page explains what is covered, what is not, and how to make a claim.

## Coverage by product

| Product | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| Voltway Home Pro 2 | 5 years, parts and labor |
| Voltway Home Lite | 3 years, parts and labor |
| Voltway Go | 2 years, parts only |
| Charging cables | 1 year on all products |

## What is covered

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and electrical failures under normal use.

## What is not covered

- Physical damage
- Lightning or power surges
- Flooding
- Unauthorized modification
- DIY hardwiring

## Installation and your warranty

- Installation by a Certified Installer Network electrician keeps your warranty valid.
- You may use your own electrician. The warranty remains valid if the installation follows the Voltway Installation Manual and is performed by a licensed electrician.
- DIY installation of hardwired units voids the warranty. This applies to hardwired Home Pro 2 units and any Home Lite that you hardwire yourself.

## Transferring your warranty

The warranty is transferable once to a new homeowner if it is registered within 30 days of the move.

## How to make a claim

1. Open the Voltway app or email support@voltway.example.
2. Describe the problem and include your charger model.
3. Once your claim is approved, a replacement unit ships within 3 business days.

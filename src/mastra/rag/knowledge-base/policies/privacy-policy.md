# Voltway Privacy Policy (Summary)

This is a summary of how Voltway handles your personal information.

## What Voltway collects

- Account details
- Payment tokens
- Charging history
- Vehicle model
- App location, only while the app is open

## What Voltway does not do

Voltway **does not sell** personal data.

## How long we keep your data

Charging history is retained for 24 months after account closure, then deleted.

## Your choices

You can request a data export or deletion by emailing privacy@voltway.example. Requests are processed within 30 days.

## Location

The Voltway app uses your location only while the app is open, for example to show nearby stations.

## Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

# Voltway Raises $180 Million Series C to Expand Its Charging Network

*Austin, Texas, March 2025*

Voltway Inc., the EV charging network and home charging company, today announced a Series C funding round of $180 million, led by Northbridge Capital.

## What the funding is for

Voltway will use the funding to grow its public charging network and to keep building its home charging hardware and the Voltway app.

"We started Voltway because charging should just work," said Maya Reddy, co-founder and CEO. "This round lets us build more reliable charging for more drivers."

## Where Voltway is today

Voltway was founded in March 2019 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas. The company opened its first 12 public stations in Austin and San Antonio in October 2020 and expanded into California in May 2023. It offers three home and portable chargers (Voltway Home Pro, Voltway Home Lite, and Voltway Go), the Voltway Plus subscription, and the Voltway app.

## About the round

Priya Nair, Voltway's Chief Financial Officer, led the company's side of the round. "Honest pricing and reliability are how we earn trust, and this investment backs both," she said.

## About Voltway

Voltway's mission is to make owning an EV as easy as owning a phone: plug in at home, top up on the road, never think about it. Learn more at www.voltway.example.

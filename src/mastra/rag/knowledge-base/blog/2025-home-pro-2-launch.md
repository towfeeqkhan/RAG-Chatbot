# Introducing Voltway Home Pro 2

*Austin, Texas, August 2025*

Today Voltway launched Voltway Home Pro 2, the next generation of its flagship home charger. Home Pro 2 replaces the original Voltway Home Pro, which launched in April 2022.

## What is new

Home Pro 2 is a hardwired Level 2 charger with up to 11.5 kW of power (48 amps). It adds about 37 miles of range per hour of charging.

Highlights:

- **A free NACS adapter in the box,** so Tesla drivers can plug in right away. Home Pro 2 also has a J1772 connector.
- **Load sharing,** which lets you run two chargers on one circuit.
- **Solar-aware charging** through the Voltway app, reading data from select inverters (Enphase and SolarEdge).
- **Off-peak rate optimization,** to shift charging to cheaper hours on your home electricity plan.
- **Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz) and Bluetooth** connectivity, plus automatic software updates.
- **NEMA 4 weatherproofing** for indoor and outdoor installation.

Home Pro 2 needs a 60-amp breaker. Professional installation through the Certified Installer Network is $199 flat for a standard install.

"Home Pro 2 is the charger we would want in our own garages," said Sara Lindqvist, Chief Product Officer.

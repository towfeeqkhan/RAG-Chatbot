# Voltway's Network Passes 2,400 Stations

*Austin, Texas, October 2026*

Voltway's public charging network has passed 2,400 stations. Here is where the network stands as of October 2026.

## The network in numbers

| Metric | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Total stations | 2,400 |
| Total charging ports | 11,800 |
| DC fast ports | 6,200 |
| Level 2 ports | 5,600 |
| US states covered | 9 |
| Canada | Vancouver and Toronto (22 stations) |
| Network uptime (trailing 12 months) | 98.2% |

The nine US states are Texas, California, Colorado, Arizona, Washington, Oregon, Florida, North Carolina, and Georgia.

## How we got here

Voltway opened its first 12 stations in Austin and San Antonio in October 2020. The network reached 200 stations in September 2022 and expanded into California in May 2023. Voltway opened its first Canadian stations in Vancouver and Toronto in March 2026.

## Reliability is the goal

Uptime over the trailing 12 months was 98.2%. Voltway's target is 99% by the end of 2027.

"Reliability first is our first value," said Luis Herrera, Chief Operating Officer. "More stations only help if they work when you pull in."

If a charger is not working, tap Report in the Voltway app. A technician is dispatched for any report, with a target fix time of 48 hours.

Stations are open 24/7. Most are at grocery stores, shopping centers, highway rest stops, and hotels. Learn more on the [Voltway Fast stations page](../products/fast-stations.md).

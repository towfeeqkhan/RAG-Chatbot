# Voltway Opens Its First Stations in Canada

*Austin, Texas, March 2026*

Voltway opened its first charging stations in Canada this month, in Vancouver and Toronto.

## What is opening

Voltway now operates 22 stations across Vancouver and Toronto. They are part of the same Voltway network that serves drivers in 9 US states.

"Drivers in Canada told us they wanted the same simple charging experience," said Maya Reddy, co-founder and CEO. "Vancouver and Toronto are our first step."

## Finding the stations

Use the Voltway app to find stations in Vancouver and Toronto with live availability, updated every 30 seconds.

## About Voltway

Voltway was founded in March 2019 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas. Its mission is to make owning an EV as easy as owning a phone: plug in at home, top up on the road, never think about it.

# Voltway Certified Installer Network: Partner Guide

Voltway runs a Certified Installer Network of about 1,100 licensed electricians in the 9 US states where Voltway operates. This guide explains what a standard installation includes, what it costs customers, and what installers need to know.

## Network overview

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Network size | About 1,100 licensed electricians |
| Coverage | 9 US states: Texas, California, Colorado, Arizona, Washington, Oregon, Florida, North Carolina, Georgia |
| Run by | Luis Herrera, Chief Operating Officer |

## What a standard installation includes

The customer pays **$199 flat** for a standard installation. It includes:

- Mounting the charger
- Wiring up to 25 feet from the breaker panel
- Commissioning the charger

## What is not included

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Permits | Vary by city, typically $50.00 to $250. Paid by the customer. |
| Panel upgrades | Customer cost, typically $1,500 to $3,500 |
| Trenching | Not included |
| Runs over 25 feet | Extra $6.00 per foot |

## Circuit requirements

| Charger | Breaker | Circuit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voltway Home Pro 2 | 60-amp | 240V |
| Voltway Home Lite | 40-amp | 240V |

If the home panel cannot support the required breaker, the installer may recommend a panel upgrade.

## Timing

- Customers are typically scheduled 5 to 10 business days after ordering.
- A standard installation takes about 2 to 3 hours.

## Installer responsibilities

1. Confirm the home's panel can support the breaker the charger needs.
2. Explain to the customer any extra costs, such as permits, panel upgrades, or extra distance, before work starts.
3. Follow the Voltway Installation Manual.
4. Commission the charger and confirm it works with the Voltway app.

## Warranty and installation

The product warranty stays valid when an installation follows the Voltway Installation Manual and is performed by a licensed electrician. DIY installation of hardwired units voids the warranty.

Customers may also use their own electrician. See the [installation guide](installation-guide.pdf) and the [warranty](../policies/warranty.md).

# Voltway Troubleshooting Guide

Use this guide for problems with your Voltway home charger (Home Pro 2 and Home Lite) or with public charging stations.

## Home charger error codes

| Code | Meaning | Light pattern | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E01 | Ground fault detected | Solid red | Unplug the vehicle, wait 30 seconds, reconnect. If it repeats, call an electrician. |
| E02 | Overcurrent | Flashing red, 2 blinks | Check the breaker has not tripped. Reduce other loads on the circuit. |
| E03 | Over-temperature | Flashing red, 3 blinks | Move out of direct sun, let the unit cool 15 minutes. |
| E04 | Wi-Fi connection lost | Flashing yellow | Restart the router, then hold the charger button for 10 seconds to re-pair. |
| E05 | Vehicle communication failure | Flashing blue | Re-seat the connector and lock/unlock the car. |
| E07 | Connector latch failure | Alternating red and yellow | Do not use. Contact support. |

## General reset

Hold the charger button for 10 seconds. You can also turn the breaker off for 60 seconds, then back on.

## Fixing each error

### E01: Ground fault detected (solid red)
Unplug the vehicle, wait 30 seconds, and reconnect. If the error repeats, stop using the charger and call an electrician.

### E02: Overcurrent (flashing red, 2 blinks)
Check that the breaker has not tripped. Reduce other loads on the same circuit.

### E03: Over-temperature (flashing red, 3 blinks)
Move the unit out of direct sun if you can, and let it cool for 15 minutes.

### E04: Wi-Fi connection lost (flashing yellow)
Restart your router. Then hold the charger button for 10 seconds to re-pair the charger with Wi-Fi. Home chargers connect on the 2.4 GHz band.

### E05: Vehicle communication failure (flashing blue)
Re-seat the connector in your car's charge port, then lock and unlock the car.

### E07: Connector latch failure (alternating red and yellow)
Do not use the charger. Contact support at support@voltway.example or 1-800-865-8929.

## Public station troubleshooting

- **Charger will not start:** Check that the app is signed in, your payment method is valid, and the connector is fully seated. Try another port.
- **Slow charging:** Common causes are a cold battery, a battery already above 80%, or a shared-power station. At some Fast 150 stations, ports 1 and 2 share power.
- **Broken charger:** Tap Report in the app. A technician is dispatched for any report, with a target fix time of 48 hours.
- **Session charged but no energy delivered:** Request a refund in the app within 7 days.

## Voltway Go

Voltway Go has no Wi-Fi and no app. Its settings are changed on its LCD display, and the Wi-Fi error code E04 does not apply to it.

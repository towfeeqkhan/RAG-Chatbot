# Voltway Home Lite

Voltway Home Lite is our budget home charger. It is a Level 2 wall charger that adds about 25 miles of range per hour of charging, and it can be plugged in or hardwired.

## Key facts

- **Price:** $349
- **Warranty:** 3 years
- **Max power:** 7.7 kW (32 amps)
- **Breaker needed:** 40-amp breaker on a 240V circuit

## Features

- **Scheduled charging:** Choose when your car charges.
- **App control:** Manage the charger from the Voltway app.

Home Lite does not support load sharing. If you want to run two chargers on one circuit, consider Voltway Home Pro 2.

## Compatibility

Home Lite works with all EVs sold in North America. It has a J1772 connector. Tesla drivers need the NACS adapter, which is sold separately for $49.00.

## Installation

Home Lite needs a 240V circuit with a 40-amp breaker. It can be plugged in or hardwired. Professional installation through the Certified Installer Network is $199 flat for a standard install. DIY installation of hardwired units voids the warranty. See the [installation guide](../support/installation-guide.pdf).

## Warranty and returns

Home Lite has a 3-year warranty covering parts and labor, and the cable is covered for 1 year. You can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. See the [warranty](../policies/warranty.md) and [refund policy](../policies/refund-policy.md).

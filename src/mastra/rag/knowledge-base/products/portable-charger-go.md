# Voltway Go

Voltway Go is our portable charger. It works on a standard household outlet or a 240V outlet, so you can charge at home, at a friend's place, or on a trip. It launched in February 2024.

## Key facts

- **Price:** $399
- **Warranty:** 2 years, parts only
- **Max power:** 7.7 kW at 240V, 1.4 kW at 120V
- **Weight:** 8 lbs, with a carry case included

## Specifications

| Spec | Value |

| Type | Portable Level 1 and Level 2 charger |

## How it works

Plug the Voltway Go adapter into a wall outlet, connect the cable to your car, and charging begins. Use the included NEMA 5-15 adapter on a standard household outlet, or the NEMA 14-50 adapter on a 240V outlet for faster charging.

## No app control

Voltway Go has no Wi-Fi and does not work with the Voltway app. Settings are changed on its small LCD display.

## Compatibility

Voltway Go works with all EVs sold in North America. It has a J1772 connector. Tesla drivers need the NACS adapter, which is sold separately for $49.00.

## Warranty and returns

Voltway Go has a 2-year warranty covering parts only, and the cable is covered for 1 year. You can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. See the [warranty](../policies/warranty.md) and [refund policy](../policies/refund-policy.md).

# Voltway Fast Stations and the Public Network

Voltway Fast stations are our public DC fast chargers. They are built for road trips and quick top-ups. Voltway also runs Level 2 public chargers at destination locations.

## Voltway Fast models

| Model | Power | Connectors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voltway Fast 150 | Up to 150 kW | NACS and CCS1 |
| Voltway Fast 350 | Up to 350 kW | NACS and CCS1 |

A typical session takes a compatible vehicle from 10% to 80% in about 25 minutes at a 350 kW station.

At some Fast 150 stations, ports 1 and 2 share power, which can mean slower charging when both ports are in use.

## Level 2 public chargers

Level 2 public chargers (up to 19.2 kW) are available at destination locations such as malls and hotels. They are a good fit when you will be parked for a few hours.

## Where stations are

Stations are open 24/7. Most are located at grocery stores, shopping centers, highway rest stops, and hotels. Use the Voltway app to find stations with live availability, updated every 30 seconds.

## How to pay

- The Voltway app
- A Voltway RFID card (free for your first card, $5.00 for a replacement)
- Credit card tap at Fast stations

## Plug-and-Charge

Plug-and-Charge is supported on most 2022 and newer vehicles. Plug in and charging starts without opening the app.

## Pricing

Rates depend on your plan. See [subscription plans](../pricing/subscription-plans.md). Voltway does not use time-of-day pricing on the public network.

## Broken charger?

Tap Report in the Voltway app. A technician is dispatched for any report, with a target fix time of 48 hours. See [troubleshooting](../support/troubleshooting.md).

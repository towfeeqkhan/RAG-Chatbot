# Voltway Home Pro 2

Voltway Home Pro 2 is our flagship home charger. It is a hardwired Level 2 wall charger that adds about 37 miles of range per hour of charging. It launched in August 2025 and replaced the original Voltway Home Pro.

## Key facts

- **Price:** $549
- **Warranty:** 5 years
- **Max power:** 11.5 kW (48 amps)
- **Breaker needed:** 60-amp breaker on a 240V circuit

## Features

- **Scheduled charging:** Set the hours your car charges.
- **Off-peak rate optimization:** Shift charging to cheaper hours on your home electricity plan.
- **Load sharing:** Run two chargers on one circuit.
- **App control:** Start, stop, and schedule from the Voltway app.
- **Automatic software updates:** The charger updates itself over Wi-Fi.
- **Solar-aware charging:** Reads data from select inverters (Enphase and SolarEdge) through the app.

## Compatibility

Home Pro 2 works with all EVs sold in North America. It uses a J1772 connector, and a NACS adapter is included in the box at no extra cost, so Tesla drivers can use it right away.

## Installation

Home Pro 2 is hardwired and needs a 240V circuit with a 60-amp breaker. Professional installation through the Certified Installer Network is $199 flat for a standard install. See the [installation guide](../support/installation-guide.pdf) for details. DIY installation of hardwired units voids the warranty.

## Warranty and returns

Home Pro 2 has a 5-year warranty covering parts and labor, and the cable is covered for 1 year. You can return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. See the [warranty](../policies/warranty.md) and [refund policy](../policies/refund-policy.md).

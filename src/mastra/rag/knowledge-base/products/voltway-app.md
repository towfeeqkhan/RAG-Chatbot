# Voltway App

The Voltway app is how you find chargers, start sessions, pay, and manage your home charger. It is available for iOS and Android. The current version is 4.8.

## What you can do

- **Find stations** with live availability, updated every 30 seconds.
- **Start, stop, and pay** for charging sessions.
- **Manage your subscription** and payment methods.
- **Control your home charger.** This works with Voltway Home Pro 2 and Voltway Home Lite only.
- **Report a broken charger** with one tap.
- **Plan trips** with charging stops.

## Requirements

| Platform | Minimum version |
| --- | --- |
| iOS | iOS 16 |
| Android | Android 10 |

## Which products work with the app

| Product | App control |
| --- | --- |
| Voltway Home Pro 2 | Yes |
| Voltway Home Lite | Yes |
| Voltway Go | No (uses its own LCD display) |

## Starting a session

1. Open the app and find a station.
2. Plug your car into an available port.
3. Start the session in the app, or use your Voltway RFID card. On most 2022 and newer vehicles, Plug-and-Charge starts the session automatically.
4. Stop the session in the app when you are done.

## Reporting a problem

If a public charger is broken, tap Report in the app. A technician is dispatched for any report, with a target fix time of 48 hours. If a session charged you but delivered no energy, request a refund in the app within 7 days.

## Subscriptions

You can switch between Pay-as-you-go, Voltway Plus, and Voltway Pro in the app. See [subscription plans](../pricing/subscription-plans.md).

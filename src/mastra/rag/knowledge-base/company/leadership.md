# Voltway Leadership Team

Voltway is led by two co-founders and four executives. This page lists each leader, their role, and a short background.

## Co-founders

### Maya Reddy, Co-founder and CEO

Maya is a former product lead at a ride-sharing company. She started Voltway after a failed road trip in 2018, when three chargers in a row were broken. She co-founded the company in March 2019 and leads it as CEO.

### Daniel Okafor, Co-founder and CTO

Daniel is a power-electronics engineer who previously worked on grid-scale batteries. As co-founder and Chief Technology Officer, he leads engineering at Voltway.

## Executive team

### Priya Nair, Chief Financial Officer

Priya joined Voltway in 2022. She led the company's Series C funding round of $180 million in March 2025.

### Luis Herrera, Chief Operating Officer

Luis joined in 2021. He runs station deployment and the Certified Installer Network of installer partners.

### Sara Lindqvist, Chief Product Officer

Sara joined in 2022. She owns Voltway's hardware products and the Voltway app.

### Tom Becker, VP of Customer Experience

Tom joined in 2023. He runs customer support and the help center.

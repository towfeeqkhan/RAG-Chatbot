# About Voltway

Voltway Inc. builds and runs an electric vehicle (EV) charging network, and makes home charging hardware to go with it. Our tagline says it simply: **Charge anywhere. Go everywhere.**

## Who we are

Voltway was founded in March 2019 and is headquartered at 1200 Brazos Street, Austin, Texas, USA. As of October 2026, about 420 people work at Voltway.

We make three kinds of things:

- **A public charging network** with DC fast chargers and Level 2 chargers, open 24/7.
- **Home charging hardware:** Voltway Home Pro 2, Voltway Home Lite, and the portable Voltway Go.
- **The Voltway app** for iOS and Android, which ties it all together.

## Our mission

Make owning an EV as easy as owning a phone: plug in at home, top up on the road, never think about it.

## Our values

1. **Reliability first.** A charger that works every time matters more than any feature.
2. **Honest pricing.** Our rates are published. We do not use time-of-day pricing on the public network.
3. **Simple by default.** Fewer steps, fewer surprises.
4. **Own the problem.** When something goes wrong, we fix it and we do not point fingers.

## Our story

Voltway started after a failed road trip in 2018. Our co-founder and CEO, Maya Reddy, pulled into three chargers in a row and found all three broken. She teamed up with Daniel Okafor, a power-electronics engineer, and Voltway was founded in March 2019.

In October 2020 the first 12 public charging stations opened in Austin and San Antonio. Since then the network has grown to more than 2,400 stations (October 2026) across 9 US states, plus Vancouver and Toronto in Canada.

See the [company timeline](company-timeline.md) for the full history and [leadership](leadership.md) for the team.

## Funding

Voltway raised a Series C of $180 million in March 2025, led by Northbridge Capital.

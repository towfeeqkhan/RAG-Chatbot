## Milestones by year

### 2019 to 2021: Getting started
Voltway was founded in March 2019. The first 12 public stations opened in October 2020 in Austin and San Antonio, and the Voltway app 1.0 followed in June 2021.

### 2022 to 2023: Home charging and subscriptions
The original Voltway Home Pro launched in April 2022. The network reached 200 stations in September 2022. Voltway Plus launched in January 2023, and Voltway expanded into California in May 2023.

### 2024 to 2025: New products and funding
Voltway Go, the portable charger, launched in February 2024. The company raised its Series C in March 2025. Voltway Home Pro 2 launched in August 2025 and replaced the original Home Pro. The Voltway Pro subscription tier launched in November 2025.

### 2026: Canada and 2,400 stations
The first Canadian stations opened in Vancouver and Toronto in March 2026. In October 2026 the network passed 2,400 stations.

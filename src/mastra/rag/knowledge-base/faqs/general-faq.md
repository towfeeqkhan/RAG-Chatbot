# Voltway General FAQ

## Compatibility

### Is Voltway compatible with Tesla?
Yes. Voltway Fast stations have NACS connectors natively. Voltway Home Pro 2 includes a NACS adapter in the box. Voltway Home Lite and Voltway Go need the NACS adapter, which is sold separately for $49.00.

### Can I use Voltway chargers with any EV?
Yes. Voltway works with all EVs sold in North America with J1772, NACS, or CCS1 ports.

## Using Voltway

### Do I need a subscription to use public chargers?
No. Pay-as-you-go works with no monthly fee. See [subscription plans](../pricing/subscription-plans.md).

### Can I charge in the rain?
Yes. All Voltway products are weatherproof.

### Does Voltway offer solar integration?
Voltway Home Pro 2 supports solar-aware charging through the Voltway app. It reads data from select inverters: Enphase and SolarEdge.

## Business

### Does Voltway offer fleet or business charging?
Voltway for Business offers hosted stations for property owners. Contact business@voltway.example. Details are not available beyond this.

### How do I host a Voltway station at my business?
Email business@voltway.example. Voltway pays for installation and shares revenue. The terms are not published.

## Company

### Where is Voltway based?
Voltway is headquartered at 1200 Brazos Street, Austin, Texas, USA. It was founded in March 2019.

### How do I contact Voltway?
Email support@voltway.example or call 1-800-865-8929 (1-800-VOLTWAY). Phone support for network emergencies is available 24/7. See [support hours](../policies/support-hours.txt).

### Where can I find the Voltway network?
Voltway operates more than 2,400 stations (October 2026) in 9 US states: Texas, California, Colorado, Arizona, Washington, Oregon, Florida, North Carolina, and Georgia. There are also stations in Vancouver and Toronto in Canada. Use the Voltway app to find stations with live availability.

# Voltway Technical FAQ

## Home chargers

### Which home charger should I choose?
Voltway Home Pro 2 ($549) adds about 37 miles of range per hour and needs a 60-amp breaker. Voltway Home Lite ($349) adds about 25 miles per hour and needs a 40-amp breaker. If you have a 40-amp breaker and do not want a panel upgrade, choose Home Lite.

### What are the main differences between Home Pro 2 and Home Lite?
Home Pro 2 has 11.5 kW max power, load sharing, an included NACS adapter, and a 5-year warranty. Home Lite has 7.7 kW max power, no load sharing, a separate NACS adapter ($49.00), and a 3-year warranty.

### How fast will my car charge at home?
Home Pro 2 adds about 37 miles of range per hour and Home Lite about 25 miles per hour. Voltway Go adds less on a standard household outlet because it is limited to 1.4 kW at 120V, and up to 7.7 kW at 240V.

### Which chargers work with the Voltway app?
Home Pro 2 and Home Lite. Voltway Go has no Wi-Fi and no app control.

### What Wi-Fi does my charger need?
Home chargers connect on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.

## Public network

### How fast is a Voltway Fast station?
Voltway Fast 150 delivers up to 150 kW and Fast 350 up to 350 kW. A typical session takes a compatible vehicle from 10% to 80% in about 25 minutes at a 350 kW station.

### Why is my charging slow?
Common causes are a cold battery, a battery already above 80%, or a shared-power station. At some Fast 150 stations, ports 1 and 2 share power.

### What is Plug-and-Charge?
It starts a session when you plug in, without opening the app. It is supported on most 2022 and newer vehicles.

### How reliable is the network?
Trailing 12-month uptime is 98.2% (as of October 2026). The target is 99% by the end of 2027.

### A charger is broken. What do I do?
Tap Report in the app. A technician is dispatched for any report, with a target fix time of 48 hours.

## Errors

### My charger shows a flashing yellow light.
That is error E04, meaning the Wi-Fi connection was lost. Restart your router, then hold the charger button for 10 seconds to re-pair.

### How do I reset my charger?
Hold the charger button for 10 seconds, or turn the breaker off for 60 seconds then back on.

### What do the other error codes mean?
E01 ground fault, E02 overcurrent, E03 over-temperature, E05 vehicle communication failure, E07 connector latch failure. See the [troubleshooting guide](../support/troubleshooting.md) and [error codes](../support/error-codes.json).

# Voltway Billing FAQ

## Plans and rates

### How much does public charging cost?
It depends on your plan. Pay-as-you-go is $0.34 per kWh on Level 2 and $0.52 per kWh at DC fast chargers. Voltway Plus ($9.99 per month) is $0.26 and $0.42 per kWh. Voltway Pro ($24.99 per month) is $0.20 and $0.36 per kWh. See [subscription plans](../pricing/subscription-plans.md).

### Is there a free trial?
Yes. New accounts get 30 days of Voltway Plus free. No card is needed for the trial.

### Do rates change at peak times?
No. Voltway does not use time-of-day pricing on the public network.

### Are rates the same in every state?
Rates apply in all US states.

### Is there an annual price?
Yes. Voltway Plus is $99 per year and Voltway Pro is $249 per year. Each is about 2 months free compared with paying monthly.

## Idle fees

### What is an idle fee?
An idle fee of $0.40 per minute applies after your grace period ends. It is charged only when the station is busy with other drivers waiting. The maximum is $20.00 per session.

### How long is the grace period?
10 minutes on Pay-as-you-go and Voltway Plus. 20 minutes on Voltway Pro.

## Cancelling and refunds

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Cancel anytime in the app. Monthly plans end at the end of the billing period. Annual plans are refunded pro rata within the first 30 days only.

### What if a charging session failed?
Request a refund in the Voltway app within 7 days. Refunds are issued as account credit, or to your card on request.

### Can I return hardware?
Yes. You have 30 days from delivery for a full refund, with no restocking fee, even if used. A prepaid return label is provided. Refunds go to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days of Voltway receiving the item. The installation fee is refunded only if the installation was not yet performed. See the [refund policy](../policies/refund-policy.md).

## Shipping and payment

### How much is shipping?
Free on hardware orders over $300. Orders under $300 pay $15.00 flat. See the [shipping policy](../policies/shipping-policy.md).

### How can I pay at a station?
Use the Voltway app, a Voltway RFID card (first card free, $5.00 for a replacement), or tap a credit card at Fast stations.

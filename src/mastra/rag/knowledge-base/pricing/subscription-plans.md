# Voltway Subscription Plans and Charging Rates

Voltway has three ways to pay for public charging: Pay-as-you-go, Voltway Plus, and Voltway Pro. You do not need a subscription to use public chargers.

## Plans at a glance

| | Pay-as-you-go | Voltway Plus | Voltway Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly fee | $0 | $9.99 | $24.99 |
| Annual price | n/a | $99 (about 2 months free) | $249 (about 2 months free) |
| Level 2 rate | $0.34 per kWh | $0.26 per kWh | $0.20 per kWh |
| DC fast rate | $0.52 per kWh | $0.42 per kWh | $0.36 per kWh |
| Idle fee grace period | 10 minutes | 10 minutes | 20 minutes |
| Priority support | No | No | Yes (24/7 phone line, under 2 minute wait target) |
| Reserve a charger | No | No | Yes, up to 30 minutes ahead |

## Pay-as-you-go

No monthly fee. Pay only for the energy you use: $0.34 per kWh on Level 2 chargers and $0.52 per kWh at DC fast chargers.

## Voltway Plus

$9.99 per month, or $99 per year (about 2 months free). Rates drop to $0.26 per kWh on Level 2 and $0.42 per kWh at DC fast chargers. Voltway Plus launched in January 2023.

## Voltway Pro

$24.99 per month, or $249 per year (about 2 months free). Voltway Pro launched in November 2025. It has the lowest rates: $0.20 per kWh on Level 2 and $0.36 per kWh at DC fast chargers. It also includes:

- A 20-minute idle fee grace period
- Priority support through a 24/7 phone line, with a wait target of under 2 minutes
- The ability to reserve a charger up to 30 minutes ahead

## Free trial

New accounts get 30 days of Voltway Plus free. No card is needed for the trial.

## Cancellation

You can cancel anytime in the app. Monthly plans end at the end of the billing period. Annual plans are refunded pro rata within the first 30 days only.

## Idle fees

An idle fee of $0.40 per minute applies after the grace period ends. It is charged only when the station is busy with other drivers waiting. The maximum is $20.00 per session.

## Good to know

- **Peak pricing:** None. Voltway does not use time-of-day pricing on the public network.
- **Where rates apply:** The rates above apply in all US states.
- **Canada:** Canadian rates are not covered in this knowledge base.

## Which plan is cheapest?

It depends on how much you charge in public. A quick way to compare: Voltway Plus saves $0.08 per kWh on Level 2 and $0.10 per kWh on DC fast compared with Pay-as-you-go. Voltway Pro saves $0.14 per kWh on Level 2 and $0.16 per kWh on DC fast.
